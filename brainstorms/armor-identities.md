# Armor identity directions

> **Brainstorm — non-canonical.** Canonical design leaves armor type's degree of
> influence and particular bonuses unresolved. See
> [`design/equipment.md`](../design/equipment.md).

Possible axes to explore without assigning fixed identities:

- mitigation profile: flat reduction, resistance, avoidance, recovery, wards,
  or conditional protection;
- attack timing: ATB charge, recovery time, interruption risk, or action cadence;
- skill interaction: costs, effectiveness, targeting, status application, or
  access conditions;
- survivability: burst tolerance, attrition tolerance, cleansing support, or
  ally protection;
- equipment tradeoffs: weight, slot pressure, offense sacrificed for defense,
  and specialized resistance;
- party role: protecting allies, sustaining oneself, acting quickly, enabling
  skills, or countering particular threat domains.

Questions for later design approval:

- Are armor types soft incentives or hard skill constraints?
- Can any character equip any armor type, and how easy is switching?
- Does a mixed armor set create a coherent identity or dilute one?
- How does armor remain relevant beside level, individual gear properties, and
  learned skills?
- How can armor guide a role without becoming an innate class under another
  name?
- Should armor kind remain a compatibility group for automatic gearing, or is
  the current grouping only temporary inventory behavior?
- Should weapon style share armor-kind labels at all? The current automatic
  gearing path couples them, but accepted design does not.

The discipline/armor affinities proposed in
[`grimoire_cycle_focuses.md`](grimoire_cycle_focuses.md) remain one exploratory
option and are not accepted character specializations.
