# Origin cycle progression questions

> **Brainstorm — non-canonical.** Accepted cycle and biome structure lives in
> [`design/cycles-and-biomes.md`](../design/cycles-and-biomes.md). Values and
> routes below remain unanswered until explicitly approved.

## Difficulty and levels

- What are the relative starting, midpoint, and ending difficulties of
  Woodland, Plains, Murky Swamp, Caves, and Desert?
- What approximate character levels are expected at biome unlocks, recruitment
  floors, Honey Badger, tower conclusions, and the next cycle?
- How wide should the viable underlevel and modest-overlevel ranges be?
- How should floor difficulty communicate progression without equating floor
  number with character level?

## Parties, equipment, and skills

- What party sizes are expected at major milestones, given five recruitment
  opportunities per milestone tier and the unresolved Party 1 story-recruit
  overflow?
- Which equipment categories and threat responses become available in each
  biome?
- Which observation opportunities teach skills needed elsewhere?
- How often should a player be expected to change equipment or skill loadouts?

## Honey Badger and detours

- Where should cleansing or another effective Eviscerate/Dire Bleed response be
  found?
- Is the answer a learned skill, equipment property, party strategy, or several
  viable options?
- Which biome should teach the response, and what signals should point the
  player toward that detour?
- What non-cleansing solutions should remain viable without allowing ordinary
  physical mitigation to erase the lesson?

## Time and farming

- How much ordinary repetition is expected while learning a biome?
- What amount of grinding feels like useful preparation rather than delay?
- How do travel, recovery, recruitment, observation, and gear acquisition affect
  expected time between milestone attempts?
- Which regression measurements can distinguish a strategic gate from a raw
  level check?
